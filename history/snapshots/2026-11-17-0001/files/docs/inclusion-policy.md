# Inclusion policy

Include organizations with evidence of work connecting AI and peptides: AI-native biotechnology companies, named AI teams in pharmaceutical or technology companies, and other relevant platforms or service organizations. Parent organizations may be represented as stubs for relationships and do not get cards. Individuals are not catalogued.

Classify peptide relevance as `core`, `adjacent`, or `infrastructure`. A full adjacent or infrastructure entry must cite published peptide-specific work or an official document establishing that use. If this cannot be established, use a stub or leave the entry out. Evidence must support the actual organization's connection; model usage by others does not by itself establish ownership or maintenance.

This directory is not a ranking, a marketplace, or medical advice. Do not add purchase links, discount codes, dosage instructions, or gray-market research peptide vendors.
