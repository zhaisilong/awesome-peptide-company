# Inclusion policy

Include organizations with evidence of work connecting AI and peptides: AI-native biotechnology companies, named AI teams in pharmaceutical or technology companies, and other relevant platforms or service organizations. Also include established pharmaceutical companies with documented peptide activity in the separate peptide landscape when the connection to AI is not established. Parent organizations may be represented as stubs for relationships and do not get cards. Individuals are not catalogued.

Classify peptide relevance as `core`, `adjacent`, or `infrastructure`. A full adjacent or infrastructure entry must cite published peptide-specific work or an official document establishing that use. If this cannot be established, use a stub or leave the entry out. Evidence must support the actual organization's connection; model usage by others does not by itself establish ownership or maintenance. For each full pharmaceutical-company entry, use an evidence-linked `directory_scope` tag: `ai_peptide` requires a source linking AI with peptide work in the relevant activity or partnership; `peptide_landscape` requires documented peptide activity and does not assert an AI connection. Separate evidence of generic AI and peptide activities does not establish a direct connection. Attribute partner work accurately and distinguish planned agreements from completed activity. Existing untagged entries remain visible as pending review rather than being silently reclassified.

This directory is not a ranking, a marketplace, or medical advice. Do not add purchase links, discount codes, dosage instructions, or gray-market research peptide vendors.
