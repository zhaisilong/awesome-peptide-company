# China peptide company survey

Reviewed on 18 November 2026. The [name index](../index/by-name.md) links to the company profiles and their sources. This survey covers three kinds of AI-related activity: work reported at pharmaceutical companies, peptide programs at discovery platform providers, and projects or partnerships at younger biotech companies. A separate section covers two companies with clinical-stage peptide programs whose AI connection is still being assessed.
